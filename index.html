How to source each field so your numbers match ours

Use this alongside your field definitions. It lists where Clay looks for each field, in the same order, so a value found by hand can be compared one-to-one with the workflow's.

For every field, record two things

The valueThe number or name you found
The source linkThe page you read it on (10-K, company site, LinkedIn page)

Regional headcount: the method

LinkedIn only shows the employees who list the company and a location, often a fraction of the workforce. So we take LinkedIn's regional mix and apply it to the full employee count.

  1. Take the account's employee count (the first row above).
  2. On the company's LinkedIn People tab, count employees by country, then group the countries into regions using the list below.
  3. Share = the region's employees ÷ all employees with a known country.
  4. Estimate = share × employee count, rounded to the nearest whole person.
  5. A region with no employees on LinkedIn stays blank.

Adding up the fields: North America already includes Canada and APAC already includes India, so don't add those twice. The rest of the world has no field, so the regions add up to a little less than the employee count.
